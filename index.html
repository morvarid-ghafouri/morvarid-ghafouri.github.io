Profile

About

Ali is an embedded systems engineer and electronics researcher based in Tehran, working at the intersection of low-level firmware, real-time operating systems, and human-machine interfaces. His day-to-day spans processor and MCU hardware design, multi-threaded RTOS programming, and Linux-based UI frameworks built with LVGL — with a habit of writing documentation that explains the reasoning, not just the rules.

Alongside his engineering work, Ali has spent over a decade in academic research on EEG-based brain-computer interfaces, contributing to a national patent for EEG-controlled wheelchair navigation and presenting at international neuroscience conferences. He also consults on ISO 13485 quality-management implementation for biomedical device manufacturers.

He holds an M.Sc. in Electronic Engineering from Shahid Beheshti University and a B.Sc. from K. N. Toosi University of Technology, both in Tehran.
