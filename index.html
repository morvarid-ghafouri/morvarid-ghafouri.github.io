Profile

About

Morvarid is a biomedical engineer based in Tehran specializing in EEG signal processing and brain-computer interfaces. As Cofounder and Head of Research & Development at Cafe IMED, she leads the development of BCI systems aimed at improving quality of life for patients with disabilities, working closely with clinical experts to bring rehabilitation technology from research into real-world medical settings.

Her research centers on translating motor-imagery EEG signals into control commands — most notably a four-way EEG-and-joystick-controlled wheelchair, the subject of a national patent — alongside work on electrical artificial hand prostheses and physiotherapy robotics. She is co-author of two 2025 book chapters on neuroengineering and next-generation BCI architectures.

She holds an M.Sc. in Biomedical Engineering (Bioelectric) from Amirkabir University of Technology and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering (Control) from Shahid Beheshti University, and has spent several years implementing ISO 13485 quality standards for medical device organizations.
